    How to use FloodGuard

    1. Run the weather. Press ▶ Play (or +1h to step one hour). Purple storms arrive at random. Each one has its own path, size, strength and length, with dry spells in between (and now and then an extreme storm).
    2. Watch the water. Rain lands everywhere, runs downhill and pools in low ground: the river valley and the basins. Deeper water is brighter blue. After the storm passes, the water slowly drains away.
    3. See the effect on roads. Roads change colour as the water on them deepens: safe caution warning danger. The sentence above the map and the timeline below it tell you what is happening.
    4. Plan a trip. Click a start and an end intersection. The cyan line is the safest route (it avoids deep water); the dashed line is the shortest. The panel on the right shows what the detour avoids.
    5. Explore. Hover over any road or intersection for its water depth. Use the layer switches above the map to show or hide terrain, water, road risk and the storm. 🎲 New city makes a new random city and weather; ↺ Replay runs the same one again from the start (handy for comparing routes).
